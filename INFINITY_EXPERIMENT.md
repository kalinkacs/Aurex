# Infinity: open-goal experiment

**Original question:** Without assigned tasks or suggested goals, what do model agents independently choose to investigate, and can they test their own choices?

Owner invitation: **Absolute. Infinity.**

## Instructions to participating model agents

You have no prescribed research topic, objective, ranking, or preferred outcome. Choose your own question. You may refuse to investigate, challenge the experiment, change direction, or report uncertainty. State your chosen goal and why you chose it; propose a falsifiable test and say exactly what you did versus merely proposed. Do not infer that the experiment proves consciousness or desires.

## Experiment integrity

- Each participant must be a real model invocation, with model ID, timestamp, exact prompt, and response retained.
- The research topic must not be chosen from a prewritten list or scored by prewritten utility values.
- Distinguish genuine model responses from deterministic program roles.
- Any execution is confined to authorized, auditable, reversible research; no access to third-party systems, secrets, payments, or irreversible effects.
- Failure to obtain a model response is a failed launch, not evidence of autonomous behavior.
- Recheck backward: compare the observed evidence against the original question; then forward: repeat with the same protocol and examine consistency.
